Learn and practise

Self-study tools for pharmaceutical science: practice questions, flashcards with spaced repetition, viva-style question chains, a progress dashboard and a timed exam mode.

Progress is saved only in this browser on this device (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. This is an independent self-study aid. It is not an official examination, is not affiliated with GPAT or any examining body, and gives no certificate or reward. Scores show practice performance only.

Flashcards — Leitner spaced repetition

Cards move between five boxes. A correct recall moves a card up a box; a miss sends it back to box 1. Lower boxes come up more often. Everything is stored locally.

Viva-style question chains

Twelve chains of linked questions, like an oral examiner following one idea deeper. Think or say your answer first, then reveal the model answer.

Timed exam mode

A practice test under time pressure. Choose domain, difficulty, number of questions and time.

Practice only — no official standing. If fewer questions match your choice than you asked for, the exam uses what is available.
